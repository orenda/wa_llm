## Findings resolved

- Known instants now require canonical UTC `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm:ss.sssZ`, checked against the actual calendar/time value. Invalid, overflowed, offset-free, and noncanonical strings fail explicitly at render, Markdown parse, and metadata decode.
- Attachment and slot field order no longer affects equality or generated bytes. Attachment arrays retain their supplied order. Canonical output covers saved, opaque, pending, unavailable, and unsupported states.

The strict timestamp grammar is accepted as an internal interchange contract. Future source adapters may normalize an already verified instant to this form. Preserve the observed timestamp display and known precision/timezone; do not infer an instant or add timezone certainty to localized/minute-only text. The canonical string's formatting does not establish the precision of the original observation. No existing deployed-format migration is claimed.
